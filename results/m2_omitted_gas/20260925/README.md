# Omitted-gas reference requirements at three closed H-pilot sources

The three 16-layer source states are preserved from the independently audited ExoInventory H pilot. `receipt.json` links the original committed bytes and their SHA256 values; no copy is relabeled as a new solve. All three CLI executions completed on CPU with x64.

Each screen retains 41 neutral gas candidates containing Al, Ca, K, Ti, Cr or P. All 41 require at least one missing absolute atomic energy reference. The result records the inequality needed to meet the declared mole-fraction target of 1e-8 at fixed reservoir potentials. No omitted-gas abundance, coupled error bound or metal-boundary displacement is inferred from those missing references. Mg and the six background-element alloy paths also lack activity/standard data.

The source pressure belongs to the selected metal-allowed planet. These records are not the independently closed metal-free branch required for a planetary appearance boundary. They neither establish a BSE material domain nor resolve global host stability.
