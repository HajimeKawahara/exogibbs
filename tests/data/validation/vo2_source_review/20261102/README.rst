VO2 source review, 2026-11-02
============================

This compact offline packet records neutral gaseous VO2 source attribution,
energy and state-assignment limitations, and the existing gas-only importance
screen. It does not adopt new coefficients or run a source substitution.

``sources.json`` identifies acquired bytes and extraction steps. Archived
JANAF tables and pages are original NIST material. ``nasa_selected_records.txt``
contains complete V, O and VO2 records from the pinned official CEA source,
with line endings normalized. Its V and VO2 lower bounds are 300 K, while the legacy
local comparison record uses 200 K; the comparison's 298.15 K decomposition
is therefore explicitly a reconstruction of that local extension.

``literature_inputs.json`` stores transcribed numerical facts and conventions;
``literature.json`` records primary versus secondary roles, uncertainty and
access limits, related ancestry, contradictions and follow-ups. Inspected
research PDFs marked unarchived in ``sources.json`` are not CI dependencies.
The raw NASA coefficients and exact JANAF nodes are checked offline.

``comparison.json`` is regenerated by ``mceg/reviews/vo2_source_review.py``.
The current 75-case archive is reused by hash; no duplicate equilibrium NPZ
is stored. Its gas-only vanadium footprint is not a predicted observational
abundance or a source-substitution response. Electronic-only level sums
omit state-specific rovibrational factors and cannot be adopted as complete K.

The official JANAF page adopts only a constant electronic ground weight of 2.
Its excited atomic V++ level analogy and hypothetical molecular level are
uncertainty illustrations, not adopted VO2 states. The RRHO heat-capacity
closure checks that reading. Its stated formation-enthalpy uncertainty is
41.8 kJ/mol, not the 4 kJ/mol assumption used by the inspected 2024 cluster
manuscript. Both distinctions are intentional parts of the evidence record.

The local-full-text update identifies ``Balducci.pdf`` and
``Gunina_2012_sup.pdf`` by SHA-256 without committing those supplied files
or their extracted text. Balducci's full text resolves the three experimental
reaction paths, their common EuO energy anchor and historical thermal
assumptions. It also reveals a selected +/-11 kJ/mol uncertainty on p5618,
compared with +/-18 in the abstract and +/-18/12 for the Table I paths.
The audit retains this discrepancy, reproduces the selected-energy algebra
and shows that both VO2 paths shift by twice a change in their common EuO
anchor. It does not claim an independent-error average or new uncertainty.

Table IV enthalpy increments are compared with JANAF; entropy is
reconstructed in the printed convention because Table IV does not
explicitly state standard pressure. No pressure-normalized Q comparison or
new thermochemical reduction is inferred. The 2012 supplement contains
mixed-oxide equilibrium data and structures, not the missing main article's
critical VO/VO2 reevaluation. Its reaction-number mismatch is recorded.
