NaOH candidate evidence
=======================

Derived offline evidence for the bounded NaOH candidate comparison.
The production baseline is curated-cos-v1; no production adoption is made.
Source facts and access records remain in the separate naoh_source_review packet.
No copyrighted full articles or new OYT data are included.

candidate.json records the balanced energy gauges, native/SI R conversion and exact coefficients.
validation.json replays 2501 temperatures and independent analytic/JAX derivatives.

See documents/mceg/naoh_candidate.rst for scientific choices and limitations.
