## ExoGibbs comparison baseline modifications

This directory contains selected neutral-gas NASA9 coefficient records in
inputs.json, exported and reformatted from the existing local modified copy
VULCAN/thermo/NASA9/nasa9_2002_E.txt. That full local source has SHA256
1ec5fe226af698ef6c900537d30c2a640b31f83483d3f6f51c47c813f8d009ee.
It has not been verified against a new official NASA download. The 47 records
(94 coefficient intervals) overlapping the existing ExoGibbs major-molecule
fixture agree in coefficients with that official-source-derived fixture;
this does not establish provenance or equivalence for the complete local copy.
The packaged NASA CEA license and attribution above are preserved here.

inputs.json also contains selected JANAF values from the packaged YKB4 tables
and the existing ExoGibbs major-molecule fixture. Their source metadata and
the incomplete acquisition provenance of YKB4 are recorded separately in
inputs.json; NASA attribution does not imply NASA authorship of JANAF data.
