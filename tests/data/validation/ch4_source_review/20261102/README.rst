Frozen methane source investigation, 2026-11-02
==============================================

This packet supports an initial CH4 source assessment without changing any
production thermochemistry. Conversation and planning records are maintained
separately in Japanese; the repository evidence is in English.

``comparison.json`` is generated by ``mceg/reviews/ch4_source_review.py`` from
existing pinned JANAF/NASA/ATcT inputs and the partition tables here.
``sources.json`` records SHA256 values, URLs, extraction details and access
limits. ``literature.json`` distinguishes source facts, reported uncertainty
definitions and follow-ups. The printed JANAF page, MM partition discussion
and historical Wenger table are short primary-source excerpts; parent PDF
hashes identify the full downloads. Ghostscript discarded irrelevant PDF
outline links when extracting the MM page; it does not supply numeric inputs.

The user-supplied Nikitin 2015 full paper and 2017 correction were inspected
on 2026-11-02. Their PDF hashes and local reference names are recorded in
``sources.json``; full PDFs and extracted text are not redistributed. The
three corrected Table 4 coefficient sets, Table 3 totals and selected Table 5
values are transcribed in ``literature.json``. The audit evaluates the
base-10 fit only through 3000 K and preserves its 0.61-0.80% excess over the
printed Table 3 totals. This unresolved representation residual exceeds the
paper's stated approximately 0.1% fit accuracy and is distinct from physical
uncertainty. The paper estimates 1-2% Q accuracy at 2000 K but does not
quantify higher-temperature errors or validate caloric derivatives to 4000 K.

``exomol_mm.pf`` and ``exomol_mm.def`` preserve downloaded bytes, including
their different numerical and completeness ranges. ``hitran_q32_20261102.txt``
is the current 1-2500 K HITRAN table. ``hapi_tips2021_ch4.txt`` is an exact
two-column extraction of the official HAPI TIPS2021 temperature/Q arrays for
M=6, I=1; it is not interpolated or relabelled TIPS2025. HAPI's pinned commit
and full-source hash are in the source manifest. The full HAPI implementation
is not imported, executed or required by offline tests.

The audit removes the proton spin product of 16 for comparison with
nuclear-spin-free thermochemistry; it does not remove rotational symmetry a
second time. Thermochemical effective Q still uses natural molar masses,
whereas the spectroscopic tables describe 12CH4. Version differences and
incomplete high-energy coverage remain unresolved. No dataset here is an
approved whole-domain replacement or an atmospheric accuracy benchmark.

The carbon footprint reuses the archived 75-case curated-default grid.
It is not a new equilibrium sensitivity calculation. No full state sum,
caloric convergence claim or production adoption is made.
